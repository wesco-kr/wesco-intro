What this video covers

Follow the supply-panel–TSP–equipment connection through installation and commissioning. The guide shows the terminal blocks and breakers behind the front door, the bottom cable entry, and the sequence from input-voltage checks to charging, output power and equipment operation.

  • Locate the terminal blocks below the breakers and the shared bottom entry for input, output and protective-earth cables.
  • Follow panel power-on, input AC-voltage verification, TSP start-up, charging and standby checks before switching on the output.
  • Review the equipment-operation test, the role of BYPASS and customer handover. Communications and SAG Viewer are optional.

This video explains the installation workflow. Actual work must be performed by qualified personnel using the current manual for the specific model and approved site procedures.
