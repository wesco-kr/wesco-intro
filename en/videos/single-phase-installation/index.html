What this video covers

Follow the installation workflow for a single-phase TSP protecting equipment control power. The guide covers horizontal and vertical mounting, INPUT and OUTPUT connections, protective earth, both terminal covers, power-up and commissioning.

  • Understand the connection from the supply to INPUT and from OUTPUT to the equipment control-power input.
  • See the terminal-block cover and additional metal cover, followed by charging and the green standby indication.
  • Review the input-branch interruption test and customer handover. Communications and SAG Viewer monitoring are optional.

This video explains the installation workflow. Actual work must be performed by qualified personnel using the current manual for the specific model and approved site procedures.
