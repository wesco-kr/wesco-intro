Protection System

Utility sag. Load normal.

TSP is installed in series between utility power and factory load. When voltage drops, the system isolates the utility path and compensates from EDLC through the inverter until recovery and synchronization.

Normal Utility

Bypass mode. Utility feeds the load through TSP with high efficiency.

Voltage Sag Event

Utility voltage drops. TSP compensation keeps load voltage stable.
